# StarWorld Contributor Notes

Start with `README.md`, `docs/method.md`, and `docs/maintenance.md`.

- Keep changes focused on the method, data pipeline, and minimal runnable examples.
- Preserve the causal and partitioning invariants in the maintenance guide.
- Keep `README.md` and `README_CN.md` synchronized.
- Write project descriptions in terms of implemented capabilities and intended use.
- Keep dataset attribution linked to the official StarNet source and its DOI.
- Place generated inputs, checkpoints, and plots under `outputs/` or `data/`.
